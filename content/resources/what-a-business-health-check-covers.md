A business health check is a structured look at how well an enterprise is run, not only how much money it made last month. Samakose scores a business across six dimensions. Each one is a place where small problems quietly become large ones.

## Finance
Do you know what comes in, what goes out and what is owed to you? Cash flow, margins, debts and the habit of separating personal and business money all sit here.

## Market and sales
Who buys from you, how do they find you, and how often do they come back? A business that depends on one buyer or one season is more exposed than it looks.

## Operations
How work gets done day to day: production or service delivery, stock, suppliers, quality and time lost to rework.

## People and governance
Who decides what, who is accountable, and how staff, partners or cooperative members are managed. Unclear roles are one of the most common hidden costs.

## Records and systems
Whether the business keeps records that someone else could read. Good records make every other dimension easier to improve, and they are what lenders and partners ask to see.

## Compliance and access to finance
Registration, tax, licences and the paperwork that lets a business open doors to banks, buyers and programmes.

## What you get back
The health check places the business in a band, from Critical to Strong by default, and shows which dimensions pull the score down. From there, the platform proposes prescriptions: specific actions with owners and dates. The point is not a grade. It is a short list of the next things worth fixing.
