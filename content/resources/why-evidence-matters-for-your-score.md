Two businesses can give the same answer to a question and deserve different confidence. One keeps signed records. The other is going from memory. Samakose treats that difference openly.

## Five levels of evidence
Every answer in a health check carries an evidence class:

- **Verified:** checked directly by an adviser or against an independent source.
- **Document-supported:** backed by a document you have uploaded.
- **Self-reported:** your own answer, with nothing attached.
- **Unverified:** an answer that could not be confirmed.
- **Missing:** no answer or no support.

## How it changes the score
By default, stronger evidence counts for more. A verified answer counts in full, and weaker classes reduce the weight of the answer. The exact weights are rules that administrators can review and change, so the method is transparent rather than a black box.

## What this means in practice
If your score looks lower than you expected, the cause may be thin evidence, not a weak business. Uploading a bank statement, a sales book or a registration certificate can raise confidence in your results without changing what you do. It also prepares you for conversations with lenders and programme partners, who will ask for the same documents.

## Start small
You do not need everything at once. Begin with the records you already have, and add more as you go. Each upload strengthens the picture.
