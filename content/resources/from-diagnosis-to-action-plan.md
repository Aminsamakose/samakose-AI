A diagnosis that sits in a drawer helps no one. The Samakose method is built to end in action.

## Step 1: Diagnose
The health check measures the business across six dimensions and records how well each answer is supported.

## Step 2: Prioritise
The score shows which dimensions are dragging the business down. Not everything needs fixing at once, so the focus is on the few conditions that matter most.

## Step 3: Prescribe
Each priority is matched to an intervention: what to do, what it needs and what it should change. Prescriptions are reviewed by a person before they reach the business.

## Step 4: Act
Prescriptions become actions with an owner and a date. Progress is tracked, and evidence of completion is attached, so it is clear what has really been done.

## Step 5: Coach and re-measure
Coaches work from the same record, and the business is re-assessed over time. Scores and KPIs show whether things are improving, and by how much.

## A habit, not a one-off
The most useful result of a health check is a routine: look at the numbers, act on the top few items, and check again. The platform is there to keep that routine going.
